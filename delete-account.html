Delete your Recordance account

You can delete your account and all your records at any time, from inside the app.

  1. Open Recordance and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (☰), then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

What is deleted: your account, your name and phone number, all synced farm records, your Farm ID and Farmer ID, and your export passports. Records saved only on your phone are cleared too. This cannot be undone.

What is kept: nothing that identifies you. Backups made by our host expire within [30] days.

Cannot open the app? Write to [support email address] from the phone number you signed up with, and we will delete the account within [14] days.
