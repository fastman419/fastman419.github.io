Export passports and verification

A passport shows what you have declared, and that Recordance issued it and it has not been changed. Recordance does not guarantee that your records are correct or that a licence is real unless the passport says we checked it. Do not share a passport you know is false. We may switch off a passport that is misleading.

Acceptable use
