Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

Recordance is a farm record book. The service is run by Ogberigbe Torishe Prince, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria. Contact: Princetorishe123@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 913 485 1960.

What we collect, and why

  • Your Google account (or Apple account in the iPhone app), only if you make an account. We receive your name and email address from Google or Apple so we can sign you in and find your account. We never see your Google or Apple password, and we cannot read your Gmail. You can use the app without an account.
  • Your phone number, only when you get a Farm ID. It identifies your Recordance account (one number, one account), so buyers, lenders and exporters can trust a Farm ID. It is not used to sign in, it is never put on an export passport, and we do not sell it or send you marketing texts.
  • Your name and title. So Papa Ledger can greet you properly, and to put your name on reports you choose to share.
  • Your farm records: sales, costs, crops, animals, workers, plots, licences, invoices and loans that you type or speak into the app. This is your business information. We use it only to run the app for you.
  • Your location, only when you tap "Set location" on a plot or take a photo as evidence. We save the coordinates in your records. We do not track you in the background.
  • Photos you add as evidence (of a plot, a harvest, a certificate). The photo stays on your phone and in the backup files you save; we never upload it. We receive only its fingerprint (a code worked out from the picture, from which the picture cannot be rebuilt).
  • Time-stamps of your entries. When your phone syncs, we keep a fingerprint of each version of each entry and photo with the time our server received it, so it can be shown later that an entry existed unchanged on that day. These stamps cannot be changed, even by you, but they are deleted with your account. A passport shows how many of your entries were stamped, never the entries themselves unless you chose to share them.
  • Photos of certificates, only if you add one.
  • Farm ID and Farmer ID, made for you when you ask for them so that others can check your farm if you share them.
  • Reminder settings, your time zone, and a notification address from your phone if you turn on reminders when the app is closed. The morning greeting can show yesterday's totals on your lock screen; you can hide the amounts in Settings, and they are always hidden when App lock is on.
  • Bank and mobile-money statements, only if you import one. The file is read on your phone and is not uploaded. Only the transactions you choose to import are saved in your records.
  • Crash reports, only if you agree. If the app breaks, you can choose to send us a short technical report (the error, the screen, the app version and your phone type). We remove names, numbers and amounts before it leaves your phone.

Where your records are kept

First on your phone, so the app works with no signal. If you are signed in, a copy is synced to our database (hosted by Supabase in Ireland, in the European Union) so you can change phones and recover your records. You can turn on App lock in Settings to encrypt the records on your phone with your PIN.

Who can see your records

  • Only you, by default.
  • People you give your passport or QR code to. A passport shows only the parts you chose to share (for example licences, plots and spray log). It stays valid for 90 days and you can stop sharing at any time.
  • Our service providers, who host the database and send sign-in codes and notifications for us. They may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Speaking your entries

If you use the microphone, your phone's own speech service (for example Google on Android or Apple on iPhone) turns your voice into text. Your phone company's privacy policy covers that step. We receive only the text. Recordance does not send your records to any artificial-intelligence service.

Your choices and rights

  • See and copy your data: Settings → Save backup.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete my data. This erases your account and all synced records from our database and clears your phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Correct your details: change them in the app, or write to Princetorishe123@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, the UK or the EU) the law may give you more rights, such as complaining to your data protection regulator. Write to us and we will help.

How long we keep data

For as long as your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your records from our live database straight away. Encrypted backups made by our host may take up to 30 days to expire.

Security

If you turn on App lock, the records on your phone are encrypted. Unlocking with your face, fingerprint or phone password is done by your phone itself: Recordance never receives your face, fingerprint or phone password. Data travels over encrypted connections. Each person's records are separated in the database by access rules. Passports are digitally signed so that they cannot be changed after they are issued. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your PIN private.

Children

Recordance is for people aged 16 and over. If a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
