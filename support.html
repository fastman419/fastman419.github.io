Common questions

Do I need internet? No. Records are saved on your phone first and upload by themselves when you are online.

Do I need an account? No. You can keep your book on your phone without one. An account (Google, or Apple on iPhone) keeps a copy online and is needed for a Farm ID and export passport.

How do I delete my account? Settings, then Delete my data. More on the account deletion page.

Is my data shared? Only what you choose to put on a passport. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms.
